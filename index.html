Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Subi is built to work entirely on your device.

What Subi stores

Every subscription you add, and your app settings, are stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device storage. Subi has no backend server and no user accounts. Nothing you enter is sent to Appserd or to any third party.

Camera and Photos

When you use "Scan a screenshot" or "Take a photo," Subi reads the text in that image using Apple's on-device Vision framework, directly on your phone. The image is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored by Subi outside of that one-time scan — it is not kept, and it does not leave your device.

Worth Trying tab

The "Worth Trying" tab shows links to other companies' sign-up pages. Subi does not track which links you tap or share your subscription data with those companies. Once you leave Subi for another app's or website's sign-up page, that company's own privacy policy applies.

Analytics and advertising

Subi does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Children's privacy

Subi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all — everything stays on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
